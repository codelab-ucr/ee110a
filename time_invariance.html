Time Invariance Demo

Pick a signal and a system. Shift the input and then apply the system, or apply the system and then shift the output. If the two results always agree, the system is time-invariant.

Convention: a positive t₀ is a delay, so shifting replaces t with t − t₀ and moves the plot right. Shifting the output means replacing every t in y(t), including any t that sits outside x(·). One mismatch proves a system is time-varying. A match for one signal and one shift proves nothing on its own: time invariance needs a match for every input and every shift.
